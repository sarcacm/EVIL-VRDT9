Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EVIL VRDT9 ("the Bot") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Bot on Discord.

1. What Information We Collect

EVIL VRDT9 collects and temporarily stores the following information:

  • User ID - Used to identify you for command cooldowns and conversation memory.
  • Display Name & Username - Used to personalize responses in the /ask command.
  • Messages sent to /ask - Stored temporarily to provide context-aware responses.
  • Command Usage Data - Logged for debugging and improvement purposes.
  • Server ID & Channel ID - Used for command functionality (e.g., /servers, /archive).

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used for the following purposes:

  • To provide and improve the Bot's functionality.
  • To maintain conversation context in the /ask command.
  • To enforce rate limits and prevent abuse.
  • To debug issues and improve performance.

3. Data Storage & Retention

  • Conversation Memory: Stored temporarily in RAM while the Bot is running. Data is not saved to any permanent database and is lost when the Bot restarts.
  • Command Logs: May be logged to files for debugging purposes. These logs are not shared with third parties.
  • Archived Messages: When using the /archive command, messages are saved to a temporary file and deleted after being sent to you.

4. Data Sharing

EVIL VRDT9 does not share, sell, or distribute your personal data to third parties. The only external service used is the Groq API for the /ask command, which processes your messages to generate responses.

  • Groq API: Your questions sent to /ask are forwarded to Groq's API to generate responses. Please refer to Groq's Privacy Policy for more information.

5. Your Rights & Control

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Clear Memory: Use the /clear_memory command to delete your conversation history with the Bot.
  • View Stats: Use the /my_stats command to see your conversation history count.
  • Opt-out: Simply stop using the Bot if you do not wish to have your data processed.

6. Server Invites

When using commands like /servers, the Bot may create server invites. These invites are only created with the appropriate permissions and are only shared in response to the command.

7. Children's Privacy

EVIL VRDT9 is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact the owner of the bot (sarcacm) immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be posted here, and your continued use of the Bot constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the Bot owner: sarcacm (ADMIRA/NORI) on Discord.
